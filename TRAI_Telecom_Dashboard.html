Mar 2026 saw the strongest single-period growth (+8.43M) for any month in the dataset — partly driven by Airtel's data-bundle subscriber migrations.

🚀 Airtel's Surge

Added ~104M subs (+27%) Jan 2025–Aug 2026. Closed gap with Jio from 78.8M to just 16.8M. First operator to hit ~492M subs outside Jio.

⚖️ Jio's Plateau

Added 42.6M subs (+9.2%) but market share fell 1.2pp as Airtel grew far faster. Jio still leads but pace of dominance is easing.

📉 Vi's Challenge

Lost 6.3M subs (−3.1%) with 8 consecutive MoM declines. Turned slightly positive in 2026 post-fundraising. Share slid from 17.9% → 15.4%.

🏛️ BSNL Stabilises

Net gain of 2.1M subs (+2.3%) after years of decline. Government-backed 4G rollout beginning to bear fruit; notable Aug 2025 surge of +1.39M.

📶 Broadband Boom

Broadband crossed 1.1B subs by Aug 2026. India added 121.5M broadband subs in just 14 months (Jun 2025–Aug 2026), +12.4%.

🔀 Rising MNP

MNP requests grew from 12.1M → 15.8M (+31%), signalling intensifying competition and consumer willingness to switch operators.

Competitive Intelligence Summary
📶 3-Player Market

Jio + Airtel + Vi account for 99.97% of wireless subscribers. India's market is effectively a triopoly with BSNL as government-backed #4.

🗺️ Regional Battles

Airtel dominates northeastern and southern LSAs; Jio controls Hindi-belt and western LSAs. Delhi remains the most competitive 3-way market.

📱 5G Acceleration

Airtel's rapid 5G expansion drove subscriber gains; BSNL's government-funded 4G rollout explains its Aug 2025 surge. Urban subscribers lead adoption.

🔄 Churn Rising

15.83M monthly MNP requests signals high competitive churn. Most likely beneficiary: Airtel, whose growth significantly outpaces organic network expansion.

🏙️ Urban vs Rural

Urban subscribers grew +18% vs rural +3.7% (Aug 2025–Aug 2026). Rural share of total telephone subs fell from 44.2% → 41.0%, showing digital divide persisting.

📊 Broadband Crossover

India surpassed 1 billion broadband subscribers in late 2025, making it one of the world's largest broadband markets — primarily driven by mobile broadband.
